# Help improve pumpkin search

These are suggested checks, not a record of a completed family review. The examples come from design titles and catalog descriptions. You may find other correct results too.

1. Open your private family link and make sure you are browsing **All designs** with filters cleared.
2. Try the searches below. Look for the examples, and check whether unrelated results appear or designs you expected are missing.
3. Open a design with incorrect details and tap **Wrong or missing search details?** Tell the organizer what you see and which search you tried. If a design is missing entirely from a search, search for it by name first so you can send the report from its preview.
4. The organizer can review reports under **Settings → Review search reports** and approve corrections. After the family confirms a search example, it can be added to the automated search checks.

| Try searching | Look for | Check that |
| --- | --- | --- |
| mario | Mario; Boo - Super Mario Bros | Harry Potter is not included |
| minecraft | Minecraft Pumpkin | Mario is not included |
| princess peach | Princess Peach | Princess Leia is not included |
| spider | Spider; Spiteful Spider | The exact title appears first |
| owl round | Animal Crossing Celeste | Animal Crossing Isabelle is not included |
| puppy floppy ears | Animal Crossing Isabelle | Animal Crossing Celeste is not included |
| jack o lantern face | Ripper; Torment; Keaton | Their previews actually show the expected kind of face |
| kitty lunar | stargazer | Its preview matches your idea of a cat and moon |

Also try words your family naturally uses, names of favorite characters, misspellings, and combinations such as a subject plus a feature. Search results can be subjective: report anything that surprises you, even if it is not in this list.
